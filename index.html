                  Add SensorLab to your home screen

                  Launch SensorLab from an app icon, like a regular app.

                  1. Open SensorLab in Chrome on your Android phone from its HTTPS website. Installation is not available from a downloaded file.
                  2. If Chrome offers Install SensorLab below, tap it and confirm. Otherwise, open Chrome’s ⋮ menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.

                  After the first successful load, the app shell can open offline. Keep the page visible while recording; Android may pause apps in the background.

                  Help & limitations

                  Requirements

                  • Chrome or Edge (Web Bluetooth). Firefox and Safari are not supported. Without Bluetooth you can still use Demo mode, history and export.
                  • Keep this page visible while recording. Browsers can throttle or disconnect Bluetooth in hidden tabs or when the screen locks. Hidden periods are logged in the session.
                  • No internet is needed or used. Data is stored in this browser profile only. Export it, because clearing browser data deletes it.

                  Recording

                  • Readings are saved continuously (every ~0.25 s) while recording, not only when you press Stop.
                  • If the app closes during a recording, the session is marked interrupted on the next launch and can be recovered. Data after the last save is lost; the app does not invent it.
                  • Exports come from stored data (History), not from the chart.

                  Device states

                  • Waiting for data: connected and subscribed, but no valid reading has arrived yet.
                  • Streaming: valid readings are arriving. Degraded: connected but stalled.
                  • Backoff: reconnecting automatically, with increasing delays. Failed: gave up; press Retry.

                  Data quality

                  • Missing counts sequence numbers that never arrived (SensorLab v2 firmware only). A gap shows that data is missing, not where it was lost.
                  • Legacy devices have no sequence numbers, so loss cannot be detected for them.
                  • Rates, jitter and timing accuracy shown are measurements of this session, not guarantees.
